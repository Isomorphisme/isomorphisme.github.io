From a square to a torus

The standard presentation of the orientable surface of genus one.

Presentation:

Read each face anticlockwise from its top vertex. A superscript −1 reverses the arrow. Match equal letters with their arrows aligned.

Count after gluing

Each filled polygon contributes one face F. Each pair of equally coloured, equally labelled sides contributes one edge E. Corners with the same number become a single vertex: count each number once for V.
